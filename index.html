Welcome to the Circuit Lab! ⚡

Build and test electrical circuits to investigate how electricity flows.

🛠 What You Can Do

  • Free Play: Add, move, rotate and connect components.
  • Symbols or Objects: Change the display without removing your circuit.
  • A/B Changeover Switch: Connect the common terminal to either path A or path B.
  • Fused Bulb: Investigate how a broken filament opens a circuit path.
  • Test Mode: Complete a series or parallel circuit challenge.
  • 5-Minute Rush: Race against the clock and score points.

💡 How to Build

  1. Add components from the left panel.
  2. Choose Wire Tool, then tap one terminal and another.
  3. In Objects mode, connect one wire to the bulb's metal casing and the other wire to its bottom metal tip.
  4. Select the A/B switch and use Change to A/B to redirect the current path.
  5. For parallel branches, choose T-Junction Tool, tap an existing wire, then connect it to a bulb terminal.
  6. Toggle a switch from the selected-component panel. Lit bulbs will glow!
