Model answer: Rubber is an insulator of electricity.

(b) Score: 0 / 3

Model answer: When it rains heavily, the water level in the metal container will rise rapidly. The wooden block with the metal sheet, which floats on water, rises. When the metal sheet touches the metal rods, the circuit will be closed and electric current can flow through the circuit, so the pump works.

(c) Score: 0 / 2

Model answer: Lengthen the metal rods. When it rains less heavily, the water level will be low / will not rise so quickly, and the metal plates on the wooden block cannot touch the metal rods. When the metal rods are lengthened, even with a low water level, the metal plates can touch the metal rods, to close the circuit to allow electric current to flow through the circuit, allowing the pump to work.
